What should a quote request include?

Your workload or use case, target hardware (GPU model or compute parts), quantity, and timeline.

What does burn-in testing actually cover?

Sustained-load testing is available on request: the card runs under heavy load while we watch for crashes, visual artifacts, and thermal problems. A card that errors or looks unstable does not ship. It does not make a used card new, and it does not guarantee future life.

Selling a used card yourself? Get a signed burn-in report for your listing.

Mail the card in; you pay shipping both ways. We run a full sustained-load burn-in test and return the card with a signed result report to put in your listing photos. Flat per-card fee, quoted before you ship. Ask about certification: Open in GmailEmail app

Card running hot? Mail-in repaste plus pad replacement, validated by burn-in.

Mail the card in; you pay shipping both ways. We repaste the GPU die, replace worn pads, run a full sustained-load burn-in test, and return the card with a signed before/after thermal report. Flat per-card fee, quoted before you ship. Ask about a repaste: Open in GmailEmail app

Buying a used card? Get a written go/no-go before you pay.

Send us the listing link before you buy. We run model-specific due diligence from our burn-in failure log plus a seller-question checklist, and send back a written go/no-go with the exact risks named. In-person test added if the card is local. Flat per-listing fee, quoted before we start. Ask about an inspection: Open in GmailEmail app

Is this listing fairly priced? Get a written fair-price verdict before you offer.

Send us the listing link before you buy. We check it against recent sold listings and the going rate for that card, and send back a written fair-price verdict plus what to offer. Flat per-listing fee, quoted before we start. Ask about a price check: Open in GmailEmail app

Dead card? Get a written fault-class verdict before you pay for a repair.

Mail the card in; you pay shipping both ways. We run the test-rig sequence: power draw, POST behavior, thermal behavior, VBIOS read, fan and VRM sanity, and send back a written verdict naming the fault class (VRM, memory, core, VBIOS/firmware, or not-the-card) plus what a repair should fairly cost. Flat diagnostic fee, quoted before you ship. Diagnosis only, no component-level repair. Ask about a diagnostic: Open in GmailEmail app

What should you buy? Get a written build sheet for your budget.

Send your budget and what the machine is for: gaming, editing, homelab, AI. You get a written build sheet: the exact parts to buy, the GPU pick justified from our weekly price watch and burn-in failure log, and where to buy each part. Flat per-sheet fee, quoted before we start. This is planning, not a card: no inventory, no waiting. Ask for a build sheet: Open in GmailEmail app

Know the exact card you want? We watch the listings and vet them before you go.

Tell us the exact card and your budget, and we watch local listings: every promising candidate gets the remote-inspection vetting, fair-price verdict, and seller-question checklist before you spend a dollar. You buy from the seller; we never touch the card, so there is no inventory, no waiting, and no markup. Flat finder's fee, quoted before the watch starts. Start a watch: Open in GmailEmail app

What happens after I request a quote?

You describe your workload, target hardware, quantity, and timeline; we reply with a sourced quote. Burn-in testing is optional and quoted separately.

Why do limited offers show prices?

Each limited offer is a single opportunity, so it carries a target price. Final price, exact condition, and shipping are confirmed after you request, and nothing is agreed until you confirm back. Custom sourcing and services are quote based.

What does the quote include?

Sourcing against your exact list. Burn-in testing is optional and quoted before we start; when run, it comes with a signed result record.

How do I know you received my request?

You send the request from your own mail app, so the sent copy is your receipt. We reply to every request with a sourced quote.
