03 / QUOTE

Send the requirement.

Give us enough detail to source against the workload. The form opens a prepared email in your mail app.

What should a quote request include?

Your workload or use case, target hardware (GPU model or compute parts), quantity, and timeline.

What does burn-in testing actually cover?

Every part we ship runs under sustained load before it leaves, and anything that errors or looks unstable does not ship.

What happens after I request a quote?

You describe your workload, target hardware, quantity, and timeline; we reply within 24 hours with a sourced quote; every part runs burn-in testing before anything ships.

Why aren't prices listed on the site?

Pricing moves too fast to list honestly. Every quote is built from current sourcing for your exact list; nothing is priced from stale inventory.

What does the quote include?

Sourcing, burn-in testing before anything ships, and the result record for every part. Testing is included, never an optional extra.

How do I know you received my request?

You send the request from your own mail app, so the sent copy is your receipt. We reply to every request within 24 hours with a sourced quote.

Useful inputs

Workload, target quantity, preferred platform, memory needs, power constraints, acceptable condition, budget range, and required date.
